Statistical Adaptive Threshold Intelligence

Mathematical dynamic baselining using Rolling Median and Median Absolute Deviation (MAD) with hysteresis damping.

Scaling Factor: 1.4826 • MAD

Mathematical Formula (MAD)

Robust Statistic
MAD = median(| xi - median(X) |)
Thresholdcritical = median(X) + k • 1.4826 • MAD

Unlike Mean ± 3σ which gets distorted by extreme outliers, Median Absolute Deviation (MAD) is immune to up to 50% contamination, preventing threshold drift during legitimate seasonal surges.
